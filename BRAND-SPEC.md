# Harrison Health Group — brand specification

Mode: **Extension / preserve existing design**. Do not replace the white, black, and luxury-gold identity or reorder approved team assets. Design read: editorial service brand; premium but readable, low visual variance, restrained motion, concise page hierarchy, high brand fidelity.

- Colors: white `#FFFFFF`, near-black `#0A0A0A`, gold `#C9A84C`; dark panels invert foreground. No purple.
- Type: Cormorant Garamond headings; DM Sans body; DM Mono micro-labels. Existing fonts loaded from Google Fonts.
- Mark: existing HH favicon and established monogram/wordmark; source of the 1200×1200 Google Ads logo is an owner-delivered asset outside this repository. Do not regenerate it or substitute generic AI branding.
- Images: retain existing CDN headshots and brand imagery; Raziya's approved regenerated headshot retains `object-position: center 15%`.
- Interaction: consistent small gold borders, readable black-on-white cards and white-on-black forms, focus indication and reduced-motion support. Native `<select>` controls explicitly style closed control and option backgrounds/foregrounds on light and dark surfaces.
- New pages: reuse heading scale, hairline gold separators, black/gold CTAs, generous but not excessive spacing. Navigation must not obscure content at phone width.
- Protected contracts: `/#coverage`, `/#why-us`, `/#team`, `/#testimonials`, `/#contact`, `/#schedule` anchors; owner-supplied phone/email, Calendly, Google review link; quote flow; ordered team imagery; admin route. Scope includes necessary corrections to false-looking claims, placeholder links, accessibility, crawlability and lead consent; public release is gated.
- Known visual risk: the home page had inaccurate “purple” map legend text despite gold fills, black-on-black text in contact cards, and slow/repetitive scroll content. Correct in preview without replacing the identity.
